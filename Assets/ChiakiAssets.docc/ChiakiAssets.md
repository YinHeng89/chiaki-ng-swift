# ``ChiakiAssets``

The app's bundled images, video colour data, the FSRCNNX upscaling shader and licence notices.

## Overview

`ChiakiAssets` is a resource target. Images, video data and the third-party shader are processed into its bundle, licence files
are copied verbatim, and ``ChiakiAssets`` is the one accessor other targets use to reach that bundle.
